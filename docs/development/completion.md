# Full application completion audit

The active objective is the complete Windows/Android application described by the technical white paper. A working prototype, successful build, or local integration test does not establish full completion. This ledger records the remaining work and the evidence required; it does not replace the product requirements.

The [pinned native transport candidate](native-transport-review.md) passes nine application fixtures, all 13 configured upstream transport cases and the upstream OpenSSL test command. Its seven source/notice trees and artifact-bound advisory lookup are exported and checked. An optional source-backed Windows MSIX passes archive/notice checks, its 24-component inventory and six guards, with zero advisory matches. The candidate now also passes [both native Windows–Android roles](native-cross-platform.md#executed-windowsandroid-result) with matching bidirectional generated-file hashes. Default adoption, complete license review, full controller/file-engine interoperability and physical-network verification remain open.

The [Android large-file follow-up](provider-range-reads-and-stop.md) records passing download and upload cases above 4 GiB on the original APK. The corrected offline fixture exposed a native-cancellation pause classification bug. Its deterministic regression fails on the original APK and passes on the fixed APK; both new cancellation cases and all 45 focused download/receiving/local-copy cases pass. This includes both [provider-client publication regressions](provider-publication-client.md). The fixed APK also passes the [actual process-death probe](android-process-restart.md), retaining its original ticket and resuming from the durable checkpoint, and all **521 selected device cases** with zero failures/skips. A post-suite APK pull failed; fresh inactive-device and exact APK-hash observations recovered that completed result. The later full-size matrix passes download and offline copy before interruption leaves the upload result missing. The same owned emulator restarts with data preserved and exact original APKs observed before newer focused validation. The restored-artifact upload rerun fails at its three-hour limit on 9 November, with one test and one failure. Full provider length was observed before timeout, but final verification was not proven. The terminal output and exact APK hashes are [preserved in the repository](evidence/android-large-upload-timeout-2026-11-09.json). Dependent validators stop without executing their device cases. The complete fixed-APK matrix and a measured publication speedup remain unproven.
