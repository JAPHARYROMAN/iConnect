# Android incoming upload write-window recovery

The Android receiving owner can review one expired ordinary upload and approve an exact new write-window deadline for its existing receipt. As on [Windows](phase-70-windows-upload-window-recovery.md), the window lasts 24 hours from preparation of the review. Confirmation does not receive bytes, create a provider document, change publication decisions or resume the sender. The computer must resume the original send separately.

## Owner workflow and authority

The incoming-copy card shows its deadline and **Review this write window…**. The foreground dialog identifies the paired computer's trusted name and full device ID, destination label/path, retained checkpoint, recorded provider name and current/proposed deadlines. Cancelling keeps the journal and copies unchanged. Confirmation requires the original single-use offer, unchanged receipt and current foreground owner context. Reviews expire after five minutes on either wall or monotonic time; rollback rejects them. The exact proposed deadline is retained, and ordinary status cannot slide it forward. The Windows review also displays its bound sending-device ID.

The original peer/pairing, grant, source checksum, private native identity/checkpoint chain, collision policy and publication fields remain unchanged. A memory-only owner approval revision invalidates an offer even if a changed grant is later restored. Current Write and Create permission, exclusions, grant expiry, OS access and quotas still apply. Hide, disconnect, owner changes, history rotation, cancellation and shutdown end earlier reviews. Invalidation cancels an active check and its provider signal; shutdown waits for it before releasing the store lease. No RPC or advertised capability can create or confirm an owner review. Scoped batch uploads retain their parent workflow.

Unconfirmed offers are bounded to 128 and are not persisted; one byte check runs per incoming store. A confirmed timestamp survives restart. Completed receipts and their reserved bytes remain retained through the approved window, preserving completion evidence when the next incoming copy is prepared. Legacy metadata without the timestamp keeps its original window, while unsupported stored authority blocks the queue and preserves staging.

## Private staging and provider publication

Before displaying a review and again at confirmation, the receiver rehashes the complete acknowledged private prefix, independently of its normal cached check. It binds the native device/inode, regular-file/single-link status, size and nanosecond modification/change timestamps, and rechecks the journal, row, policy and context between bounded reads. Long reads run outside the owner gate. Normal explicit receiving still checks its prefix afterward.

A recorded provider copy follows a separate read-only path: validate the original destination/parent/document/name, read the complete expected bytes and compare the source hash, and bind the seekable regular descriptor's native version. Confirmation repeats this read. Original finalization subsequently verifies the same recorded document; it cannot rewrite it. Missing IDs, incomplete/changed copies or uncertain publication do not acquire authority to create another provider document. This review does not repair those decisions or recover absent staging.
